## From idea to useful AI

I'm an AI Product Engineer with a product mindset and a curiosity for what AI makes possible. My focus is on building experiences where models, tools, and people work together to get meaningful work done.

I enjoy the whole journey: understanding the problem, shaping the experience, connecting the systems, and refining the details. Complex problems, continuous learning, and creative collaboration are what keep me building.

## How I approach building

- **Start with the user.** Define the problem and what a successful outcome looks like before choosing the technology.
- **Give agents useful context.** Design around the information, tools, and boundaries needed to complete the task.
- **Make the work visible.** Keep progress, decisions, and results understandable to the person using the product.
- **Build a tight feedback loop.** Prototype, evaluate real behavior, and improve with evidence.
- **Care about the experience.** Reliability and thoughtful design matter as much as the underlying intelligence.
